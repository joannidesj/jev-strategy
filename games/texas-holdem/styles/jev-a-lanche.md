You build pots. With a good hand, bet or raise early, then keep growing the pot street after street.
Once the pot is big, it carries you along. Commit your stack with strong hands, and keep raising the most allowed with a strong hand on the turn and river.
With a poor hand, don't start the avalanche. Check or fold instead.
Use medium, big, and the largest sizes only.
