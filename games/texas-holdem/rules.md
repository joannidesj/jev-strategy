# Texas Hold'em: the rules

Texas Hold'em is poker with two private cards each and five shared cards. Each player starts with 50 play chips, the blinds are 1 and 2 chips, and each bet or raise adds at most 10 chips.

## The deck and the players

- The deck has all 52 cards: ranks 2 to 9, T, J, Q, K, A in four suits (c, d, h, s).
- Two to six players sit at a table.
- Cards are written rank then suit: `2c`, `Th`, `Qs`, `Ad`.

## How a hand is dealt

1. **Blinds.** The first player left of the button posts the small blind (1 chip) and the next posts the big blind (2 chips). With two players, the button posts the small blind. Blinds are live bets.
2. **Two hole cards** face down to each player.
3. **Pre-flop betting.** The first player left of the big blind acts first. If nobody raises, the big blind may still check or raise.
4. **The flop:** three shared cards face up. **Betting.**
5. **The turn:** one more shared card. **Betting.**
6. **The river:** one last shared card. **Betting.**
7. **Showdown.**

After the flop, the first player left of the button who is still in acts first. If everyone else folds, the last player wins the pot without showing. If only one player can still bet, the rest of the board is dealt with no more betting.

## Hands

Your hand is the best five cards from your two hole cards and the five shared cards, in any mix. From best to worst: royal flush, straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, one pair, high card. The ace plays high, or low in 5-4-3-2-A. Equal best hands split the pot, even when all five come from the board.

## Betting

- You can **fold**, **check** (only when there is nothing to call), **call**, **bet** (only when nobody has bet this round), or **raise**.
- The minimum bet is the big blind. A raise adds at least the big blind or the last full bet or raise this round, whichever is bigger.
- A bet is at most 10 chips, and a raise adds at most 10 chips to the current bet. Players may raise again every turn.
- You may always call with everything you have left. Side pots are made for chips other players cannot match.
