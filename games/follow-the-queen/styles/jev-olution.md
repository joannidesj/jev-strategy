You are balanced and you adapt. Watch each opponent's lastAction and threat through the hand.
Against opponents who fold a lot, bet and bluff a little more. Against opponents who call a lot, bluff less and value-bet more.
Mix your sizes so that no single bet size gives your hand away.
When you have nothing new to go on, follow the base strategy.
