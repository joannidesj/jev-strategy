You always argue the case for the bluff. Before each decision, ask what story your face-up cards tell and whether the opponents will believe it.
When you show a Queen, a wild rank, or a pair and few opponents remain, a big bet or all-in is a good argument.
Drop the bluff at once when an opponent's threat is high or they call every bet.
Use small bets when you have a real hand, and big or all-in bets when you tell the story.
