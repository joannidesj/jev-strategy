# Follow the Queen: the rules

Follow the Queen is a five-card stud poker game with wild cards. Each player starts with 50 play chips, and each bet or raise adds at most 10 chips.

## The deck and the players

- The deck has 32 cards: ranks 7, 8, 9, T, J, Q, K, A in four suits (c, d, h, s).
- Two to six players sit at a table.
- Cards are written rank then suit: `7c`, `Th`, `Qs`, `Ad`.

## How a hand is dealt

1. **Ante.** Everyone dealt in pays the ante into the pot. The ante is dead money: it is not a bet.
2. **Two cards face down** to each player.
3. **Betting round 1.**
4. **One card face up** to each player still in. **Betting round 2.**
5. **A second face-up card.** **Betting round 3.**
6. **A third face-up card.** **Betting round 4.**
7. **Showdown.**

Every player ends with five cards: two down and three up. Nothing is burned. Players who fold get no more cards.

### Who acts first

- In round 1 the first player to the left of the button acts first.
- In rounds 2 to 4 the player whose face-up cards make the best showing hand acts first. Showing hands rank only as three of a kind, then pair, then high card. Straights and flushes do not count here. Wild cards count.
- Ties go to the player closest to the left of the button. All-in players are skipped.

## Wild cards

- **Queens are wild.** They are always wild, wherever they are.
- **Follow the Queen.** When a Queen is dealt face up, the next non-Queen face-up card dealt makes its rank wild for the rest of the hand. Every card of that rank is wild, face up or face down, for every player.
- The follow card can come later in the same round or in a later round.
- A new face-up Queen cancels the previous follow rank immediately. Until the next non-Queen face-up card appears, only Queens are wild.
- A Queen followed straight away by another Queen keeps the follow pending.
- If the last face-up card of the hand is a Queen, only Queens are wild at showdown.
- Face-down cards never change which rank is wild.

Example: a face-up Queen, then a face-up 9. Now Queens and every 9 are wild. If a second face-up Queen arrives, 9s stop being wild until another non-Queen face-up card is dealt.

## Hand ranks, best first

1. Five of a kind
2. Royal flush (A K Q J T, same suit)
3. Straight flush
4. Four of a kind
5. Full house
6. Flush
7. Straight
8. Three of a kind
9. Two pair
10. Pair
11. High card

- Straights run A K Q J T, K Q J T 9, Q J T 9 8, J T 9 8 7, and T 9 8 7 A, where the Ace plays as a 6. There is no wrap-around: K A 7 8 9 is not a straight.
- The Ace is high everywhere else.
- A wild card becomes whatever rank and suit gives the best hand, even a copy of a card you already hold.
- A hand made with wild cards ranks the same as the same natural hand. Suits never break ties. Equal hands split the pot.
- Ties within a category are broken by the ranks involved: the rank of the made hand first, then the kickers from high to low.

## Betting

- You can **fold**, **check** (only when there is nothing to call), **call**, **bet** (only when nobody has bet this round), or **raise**.
- A bet must be at least the minimum bet. A raise must add at least the minimum bet, or the size of the last full bet or raise this round, whichever is larger.
- A bet is at most 10 chips, and a raise adds at most 10 chips to the current bet. Players may raise again every turn.
- You may always call or bet with everything you have left (**all-in**) when it is less than the amount needed. An all-in that is less than a full raise does not let players who already acted raise again.
- A round ends when every player still in, and not all-in, has acted since the last full bet or raise and has matched the current bet.
- All-in amounts that other players cannot match go into side pots. Each pot goes to the best hand among the players who can win it.
- If a split pot has an odd chip, it goes to the winner closest to the left of the button.

## Ending the hand

- If everyone else folds, the last player wins the whole pot at once and does not have to show their cards.
- If at most one player still in the hand is not all-in, the remaining cards are dealt with no more betting, then the showdown happens.
- At showdown the remaining hands are turned face up and the best five-card hand wins.
